Bear Viewing & Wildlife Safaris

Get up close (but safely!) with Alaska's "Big Five": bears, moose, caribou, Dall sheep, and wolves. Our expert local guides know the secret spots for the best photography and observation without disturbing the natural habitat.

  • Guided by certified naturalists
  • Premium optics and gear provided
  • Small group sizes for intimate experiences

Glacier & Fjord Day Cruises

Hear the thunderous roar of calving glaciers and spot orcas breaching in the frigid waters. Our luxury catamarans navigate deep into fjords where larger ships simply cannot go.

  • Guaranteed glacier viewing
  • Heated indoor viewing decks
